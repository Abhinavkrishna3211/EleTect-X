EleTect X — Geophone Bench Excitation Tool

Drives your laptop/speaker to produce a controlled, repeatable mechanical stimulus for cross-checking the SM-24 → INA333 → ADS1115 → STA/LTA chain — a companion to, not a replacement for, a real human stomp test.

Coupling matters more than volume. This only excites the geophone if the speaker is mechanically coupled to the same surface the sensor is coupled to — set your laptop (or an external speaker) directly on the desk/floor the geophone is resting on or touching, not across the room. Airborne sound alone at these frequencies does very little.
Small speakers roll off hard below ~60–100 Hz. Elephant footfall centers around ~20–24 Hz (ADR 0001) — most laptop speakers can't reproduce that acoustically at all. What still gets through at low frequency is mostly driver/chassis motion coupling into the desk, not true low-frequency sound — real, but weaker and less linear than the tool's frequency readout implies. Treat this as a repeatability/sanity check, not a calibrated shaker table. If you have an external subwoofer or bass speaker, use it instead of the bare laptop speaker for anything below ~60 Hz.

Repeated thump — closer proxy for footfall's transient shape

A continuous tone is a poor stand-in for a footfall's actual shape (a sharp transient, not a sustained oscillation). This fires short enveloped bursts at a set rate instead — closer in character to a repeated stomp, still fully synthetic.
